How I can help

I’ll help you work out which flats are worth viewing.

01 / SHORTLISTING

Homes that fit your plans

I’ll narrow the search down to flats that suit your budget, preferred town and timeline, so we can spend our time viewing the ones that make sense for you.

02 / PRICE

Know what you’re paying for

I’ll check recent transactions and the flat’s condition with you, then explain what I think is a reasonable offer and negotiate with the seller’s agent.

03 / RESALE

Think about your next sale too

We’ll consider the remaining lease, location and layout, as well as what future buyers may look for. Nobody can promise a future selling price, but these are worth checking before you buy.

The buying process

What happens when you buy with me?

I’ll explain each step and what you need to prepare, from our first conversation to collecting your keys.

01

We work out your budget

We’ll go through your HFE letter, loan, cash and CPF, including any sale you need to complete first.

02

I shortlist homes for you

I’ll look for suitable flats and explain why I’ve shortlisted each one. We’ll adjust the search as I get to know what you like.

03

We go for viewings together

We’ll check the layout, condition, surroundings and remaining lease. I’ll point out things you might have missed and help you compare the flats we’ve seen.

04

I help you negotiate

When you find a flat you like, we’ll compare recent transactions and agree on an offer you’re comfortable with. I’ll negotiate the price and terms with the seller’s agent and keep you updated along the way.

05

I guide you through to completion

I’ll help with the OTP, resale application, deadlines and final inspection, and keep you updated until handover.

Common questions

Before we get started

Can I start if I’m still exploring?

Of course. Tell me roughly what you have in mind and we’ll work out your options together.

Do I need an HFE letter?

You need a valid HFE letter before obtaining an OTP. If you haven’t applied yet, let me know and we’ll cover that early.

What if I’m selling a home too?

We’ll work out your sale proceeds and plan the purchase and handover around your sale.

What are your fees?

I’ll explain the service and we’ll agree on commission before you sign an agency agreement.
