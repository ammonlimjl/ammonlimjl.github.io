Why I mentor

I know what it’s like to work hard without a clear direction.

When I started out, I had the passion and wanted to do well. What I was missing was direction. I was putting in effort without knowing where to focus it.

Finding SEA changed that for me. I learnt how to turn that effort into real work with clients and completed transactions.

That’s the support I want to offer you: someone to work through the next step with, especially when everything is new.

Working with me

You’ll have someone beside you as you learn.

01

One-to-one mentoring

You’ll be personally mentored by me, with daily check-ins and weekly follow-ups to talk through your progress, questions and next steps.

02

Support through actual cases

Through SEA’s tagging system, we’ll provide cases you can work on alongside us. I’ll guide you through the case as it progresses, so you can learn by doing.

03

Help getting started with clients

We’ll provide leads at the start, then guide you on generating your own. The aim is to help you build experience and a financial base while you work towards handling your own cases.

The people I hope to work with

Ambition matters. So does how you treat people.

I’d love to work with someone who wants to succeed and is willing to put in the work, but also cares about making other people’s lives better.

Providing for your family, looking after your clients and giving back when you can are values that matter to me. If they matter to you too, I’d like to get to know you.
