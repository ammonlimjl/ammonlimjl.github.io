The latest from the HDB market

Housing announcements worth knowing about, whether you’re buying, selling or simply keeping an eye on the market.

Selected HDB updates

Updated 1 October 2026
BTO construction24 September 2026

More automation planned for BTO construction from 2027

HDB plans to introduce smart passenger and material hoists at about half of BTO sites tendered from 2027, and expand trials of floor-screeding robots to at least four more projects. These are planned construction improvements, rather than a change to the completion date of every existing project.

Source: HDBRead on HDB ↗
Buying eligibility22 August 2026

Higher income ceilings for eligible HDB buyers

For HFE applications from 24 August 2026, the monthly household income ceiling rises to S$16,000 for eligible families buying a new subsidised flat, buying resale with the CPF Housing Grant, or taking an HDB loan. The corresponding ceiling for eligible singles aged 35 and above buying a subsidised flat or taking an HDB loan rises to S$8,000.

Source: HDBRead on HDB ↗
Upcoming BTO22 August 2026

Next BTO launch moves to November 2026

The next sales exercise has moved from October to November. HDB plans to offer about 7,960 flats across Bedok, Geylang, Sembawang, Tengah, Toa Payoh and Yishun, including Community Care Apartments in Toa Payoh. Buyers need a valid HFE letter when applying.

Source: HDBRead on HDB ↗
Moving to HDB27 July 2026

15-month wait removed for certain private-property owners

Private-property owners and former owners no longer face the 15-month wait when buying a non-subsidised HDB resale flat without an HDB loan. An HFE letter is still required. The separate 30-month wait for subsidised purchases or an HDB loan remains, so the financing route matters.

Source: HDBRead on HDB ↗
Resale market23 July 2026

HDB resale prices fell 0.3% in Q2 2026

HDB’s resale price index declined 0.3% in the second quarter, following a 0.1% fall in the first quarter. This is a market-wide measure: the price of an individual flat still depends on its location, remaining lease, condition and comparable transactions.

Source: HDBRead on HDB ↗
