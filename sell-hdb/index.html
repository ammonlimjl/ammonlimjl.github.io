Common Questions

Selling Your HDB, Answered

When can I sell my HDB flat?
You need to meet the Minimum Occupation Period and HDB’s other resale conditions. Check the eligibility information for your flat in My HDBPage; I can help you understand what to prepare.
How long does it take to sell an HDB flat?
It depends on the asking price, demand and your flat’s condition. Once a buyer is found, there are further steps before the resale application. Completion is generally about eight weeks after HDB accepts that application.
How much is my flat worth right now?
Start with the free price estimate, which uses resale transaction data for your town and flat type. I can then review recent comparable sales and your flat’s condition with you to suggest an asking range.
What does it cost to sell through you?
I’ll explain the service and we’ll agree on the commission and payment terms before you sign an agency agreement.
Can I sell my flat and buy the next one at the same time?
Yes, we can plan both transactions together. We’ll review your financing and handover dates, including whether temporary accommodation or an extension of stay may be needed.
